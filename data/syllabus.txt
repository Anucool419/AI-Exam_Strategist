The Complete Machine Learning
Curriculum
A Comprehensive Guide from Fundamentals to Generative AI
2026 Edition
Chapter 1: Foundations & Supervised Learning
Topics in this chapter focus on mapping inputs to known outputs using labeled datasets.
Linear Models:
Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Polynomial Regression, GLMs.
Tree-Based Models:
Decision Trees, Random Forests, Gradient Boosting (XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost).
Distance & Support Vectors:
K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), Support Vector Machines (SVM), Kernel Trick.
Evaluation Metrics:
MSE, MAE, R-Squared, Accuracy, Precision-Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC.
Chapter 2: Unsupervised Learning & Preprocessing
This chapter explores finding hidden patterns in data without explicit labels.
Clustering:
K-Means, K-Medoids, Hierarchical Clustering (Agglomerative), DBSCAN, Gaussian Mixture
Models (GMM).
Dimensionality Reduction:
Principal Component Analysis (PCA), t-SNE, UMAP, Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA).
Association & Anomaly:
Apriori Algorithm, Eclat, Isolation Forests, Local Outlier Factor (LOF).
Chapter 3: Deep Learning (Neural Networks)
Topics include the architecture and optimization of biologically inspired models.
Architecture Fundamentals:
Perceptrons, Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLP), Activation Functions (ReLU, Sigmoid, Tanh,
Softmax).
Optimization:
Backpropagation, Gradient Descent (SGD, Adam, RMSProp), Weight Initialization (Xavier, He).
Regularization:
Dropout, Batch Normalization, L1/L2 Regularization, Early Stopping.
Chapter 4: Natural Language Processing (NLP)
This chapter covers techniques to process, analyze, and generate human language.
Text Preprocessing:
Tokenization, Stopword Removal, Stemming, Lemmatization, Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging.
Vectorization:
Bag of Words, TF-IDF, Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe, FastText).
Sequence Modeling:
RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs, Bidirectional RNNs.
Modern Architectures:
Attention Mechanisms, Transformers (Encoder-only, Decoder-only), BERT, RoBERTa.
Chapter 5: Computer Vision (CV)
Topics focused on enabling machines to see and interpret visual data.
Core CNNs:
Convolutional Layers, Pooling (Max/Average), Stride, Padding, Fully Connected Layers.
Famous Architectures:
AlexNet, VGG, ResNet (Residual Connections), Inception, MobileNet, EfficientNet.
Tasks:
Object Detection (YOLO, R-CNN), Image Segmentation (U-Net, Mask R-CNN), Face Recognition.
Chapter 6: Generative AI & LLMs
The cutting edge of AI focusing on the creation of new content.
Generative Models:
Variational Autoencoders (VAEs), Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs), Diffusion Models.
Large Language Models (LLMs):
GPT-3/4, T5, Llama, PaLM. Fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA), Prompt Engineering.
Advanced Frameworks:
Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), LangChain, LangGraph (Agentic Workflows), Vector
Databases (Pinecone, Milvus).
Chapter 7: Reinforcement Learning
Learning through trial and error to maximize rewards.
Concepts:
Markov Decision Processes (MDP), Bellman Equation, Rewards, Policies.
Algorithms:
Q-Learning, SARSA, Deep Q-Networks (DQN), Policy Gradients, PPO, Actor-Critic.
